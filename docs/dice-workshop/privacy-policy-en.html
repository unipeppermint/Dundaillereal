Privacy Policy for Rollweave

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Rollweave (“the App”) and explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Handle

Rollweave is an offline dice and tabletop game app. No account or registration is required.

The App does not automatically transmit your player names, custom games, game history, or preferences to developer-operated servers. The current version does not include advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking.

To support gameplay, rule editing, and progress recovery, the App stores the following information on your device:

  • Player names you enter.
  • Names, rule settings, and identifiers of games you create or import.
  • Favorites, current game progress, game history, scores, and personal bests.
  • Preferences such as sound and haptic settings.

This information is used locally to provide the App’s features. You may use nicknames; real names are not required.

2. Local Storage and Device Backups

App data is stored within the App’s storage on your device. The App may retain local backups and copies of unreadable data to support recovery and help prevent data loss.

The App does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization. If you enable iCloud device backups or computer backups, App data may be included and restored through those services, depending on your settings and Apple’s applicable policies.

3. Importing and Sharing Files

When you choose to import a game file, the App reads the selected file, validates its contents, and saves the game rules locally.

When you choose to share a game, the App uses the system share interface to export a game file or rules image. Exported game files contain the game name, rule settings, and necessary format information. They do not contain separate player lists or game scores. If you include personal information in a game name, that information will be included when you share it.

You choose the recipients and services used for sharing. Email, messaging, and file-storage services process shared content under their own privacy policies. The App does not automatically send your game data to these services.

4. Device Permissions

The current version does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone, and does not request advertising tracking permission.

File imports use the system file picker to access files you select. Dice sounds involve audio playback only; the App does not record audio.

5. Information You Provide When Contacting Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to provide.

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, investigate issues, and provide related follow-up support. We do not use support emails for advertising or marketing. Email communications are transmitted and stored by the relevant email service providers.

Please avoid sending sensitive information unrelated to your request.

We retain support correspondence only as long as necessary to address your inquiry and related follow-up matters, unless longer retention is required by law. You may request deletion by contacting us at the address below.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information or provide locally stored game data to advertisers or data brokers.

We do not proactively disclose personal information we hold to third parties, except as necessary to handle support communications, at your direction, or when required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can remove saved games using the App’s delete function. Deleting a game does not automatically remove existing game-session snapshots, historical scores, or local recovery copies.

To remove all locally stored App data, use the Delete App option in iOS. Using Offload App may retain documents and data.

Files or images previously exported to Files, Photos, or other services must be managed or deleted separately. Copies in device backups must also be managed through the relevant backup service.

We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App does not require users to provide their age, real name, or contact information, and does not proactively collect children’s personal information through the App.

If you believe a child has provided unnecessary personal information through a support email, please contact us so we can review and address its deletion.

9. Security

The App relies on iOS application isolation and system storage protections to help protect local data. Local backups also help reduce the risk of accidental data loss.

Please protect access to your device and carefully consider who you share files with. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or information-handling practices change. We will update the date shown above.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for relevant changes.
