Privacy Policy for Rollweave

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This policy applies to Rollweave: Slot Atelier (“Rollweave” or “the App”). It describes local gameplay data, online content, push notifications, and advertising measurement.

1. Local Gameplay and Storage

The native dice games and rule workshop can be used without an account. Player names, custom game rules and identifiers, favorites, progress, scores, history, and sound and haptic preferences are stored on your device. The native game storage does not automatically upload these records to our servers. You may use nicknames.

The App also saves the last successfully loaded in-app webpage URL for use when a later startup-link request fails. This URL may include its path and query parameters. The in-app browser retains website data, such as cookies and local storage, to support website sessions. iOS device backups may include App data, depending on your backup settings.

2. Startup Requests and Web Content

At launch, the App requests a destination URL from ketwtyajxw.top. The request contains a fixed application identifier in a field named “username”; this field is not your personal username. As with other network requests, the receiving service can see your IP address and technical request information.

If a valid destination is available, the App opens it in an in-app browser. Otherwise, it may use a previously saved URL or show the native game interface. Online pages may make their own requests and process website usage, cookies, account information, or information you choose to enter. The data involved depends on the website and features you use. Review the privacy notices on those pages before providing information.

Some webpage actions open your system browser. Those destinations and browser services process information under their applicable privacy notices. The App’s native code does not automatically attach your local game records to these links.

3. Firebase Push Notifications

The App uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service to register for and receive push notifications. This involves an app installation identifier, APNs and FCM registration tokens, and technical information needed to operate and diagnose messaging. These identifiers help route messages to an app installation.

The current native implementation does not associate the FCM token with a signed-in game account or upload it to a separate developer-operated user database. Firebase nevertheless processes messaging registration information. This integration does not include the Google Analytics SDK.

You can allow or disable notification alerts in iOS Settings → Notifications → Rollweave. Declining or disabling alerts does not prevent access to native gameplay and does not by itself disable Firebase initialization or all messaging registration processing.

See Firebase Privacy and Security and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Meta App Events and Tracking Choices

The App uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure app activation and advertising performance. When a webpage sends a supported event to the native bridge, the App forwards purchase, add-to-cart, add-to-wishlist, or completed-registration events to Meta. Monetary events include an amount and currency. These event names do not mean that the native dice games require purchases or registration.

The native event bridge forwards only the supported event name and validated amount and currency fields. It does not forward the webpage’s optional name, phone, email, or user-ID fields to Meta. The SDK may also process app and device identifiers, technical information, diagnostics, and network information such as an IP address, as described in Meta’s policies and SDK disclosures. Event data may be associated with a device or other identifiers.

The App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you authorize tracking, the App enables advertising identifier (IDFA) collection for advertising measurement and attribution. You can change this choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (or Privacy → Tracking on older iOS versions).

If tracking is denied, restricted, or not yet authorized, the App does not enable IDFA collection. Refusing tracking does not prevent access to the App. Once the permission status is determined, events may still be sent under the SDK’s restricted settings, and Apple’s SKAdNetwork may support privacy-preserving attribution. Refusing ATT is not a switch that disables all network requests or all analytics. The App’s manually generated events are temporarily queued in memory while the initial ATT choice is unresolved.

See Meta’s Privacy Policy for information about Meta’s handling of data and available controls.

5. Other Permissions, Imports, and Sharing

The native implementation does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access. File imports use the system picker and access files you select. Dice sounds use audio playback; the native app does not record audio.

Sharing a game exports its name, rules, and format information through the system share sheet. It does not include a separate player list or score history. Personal information you include in a game name will be included in that export. You choose the recipients and services, which process shared information under their own policies.

6. Recipients and Purposes

Information is processed by the startup-link service and websites you visit to provide online content; by Apple and Google/Firebase to support notifications; and by Meta for the measurement described above. Support email providers process correspondence you send us. These services may process information outside your country according to their policies.

The native App does not send your locally stored game library, player names, or game scores to Meta or Firebase. This does not exclude the separate web events, identifiers, and technical information described in this policy. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

7. Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

You can delete saved games in the App. This does not automatically remove earlier session snapshots, historical scores, or recovery copies. To remove the App’s local data, cached URLs, and its browser storage, use iOS Delete App; Offload App may retain data. Separately manage exported files, device backups, and data retained in external browsers.

Deleting the App does not automatically delete information already processed by websites, Firebase, Meta, or other recipients. Their retention periods and deletion controls are described in their policies. We do not promise a single retention period for all these services. You may contact us about data access, correction, or deletion; we will address requests within our control and help identify the relevant service where possible. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

8. Support and Children’s Privacy

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use support correspondence to investigate issues and respond, not for advertising. We retain it as needed for the inquiry and related follow-up, or as required by law. You may request its deletion.

Native gameplay does not require a real name or age. Online services have their own eligibility requirements. If you believe a child’s personal information has been provided through the App or a related service, contact us so we can investigate and address information within our control.

9. Security and Policy Updates

The App uses iOS application isolation and system storage protections. Protect access to your device and consider what information you enter on websites or share. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

We may update this policy as the App and its data practices change and will revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
