Privacy Policy

Rollweave: Slot Atelier

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rollweave: Slot Atelier ("Rollweave," "we," "us," or the "App") handles information when providing dice games, rule editing, access to this policy, push notifications, and advertising measurement. It also explains the choices available to you.

1. Local Gameplay Data

The App's native game features do not require an account. Player nicknames, game rules, favorites, progress, scores, history, and sound and haptic preferences are stored on your device. The App does not automatically upload these records to our servers, Meta, or Firebase.

You may use a nickname instead of your real name. Depending on your system settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Apple.

2. Privacy Policy Address and Network Access

At launch, the App requests the current privacy policy address from ketwtyajxw.top. The request does not include player nicknames, gameplay records, or scores.

The address service and the service hosting the policy page receive information needed to process network requests, which may include your IP address, request time, and technical information supplied by the browser or network request. This information is used to deliver the page, troubleshoot access problems, and protect the service.

The returned address is used only to display the App's privacy policy. It does not provide account registration, shopping, deposits, or additional game services. The App may save the last successfully visited policy address for later access. The browsing component may also store cached page content and website data.

The address lookup service and the document host are separate recipients of network requests. The browsing component may retain website cookies and storage; deleting the App removes its local browser data but does not delete the host's server logs. A different browser's data must be managed in that browser.

3. Meta Advertising Measurement and Tracking Permission

The App uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure app installs and activations attributable to our advertising campaigns. This helps us understand which promotional channels are effective.

We enable this measurement only after you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. Information processed for this purpose may include:

  • App installation or activation events and their timestamps;
  • The app identifier, app version, and device and operating system technical information;
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) and related identifiers used by the SDK; and
  • Network information, such as your IP address.

Meta may associate this information with information it holds from other apps or websites for advertising attribution and measurement.

We do not use this integration to report player nicknames, game rules, scores, file contents, purchases, shopping cart actions, wish list actions, or account registration events.

If you have not granted tracking permission, deny permission, or are subject to system tracking restrictions, we do not enable this Meta tracking or installation and activation event reporting. We do not use alternative identifiers to bypass your choice. Declining tracking does not prevent you from playing the native games, reading this policy, or choosing whether to receive notifications.

You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Withdrawing permission stops future processing under this consent but does not automatically delete information already sent.

For details about Meta's processing and privacy controls, see the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Push Notifications

The App uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for version updates, feature updates, and important service notices. We do not use these notifications to deliver third-party advertisements.

To register an app installation and deliver messages, these services process installation identifiers, APNs and FCM registration tokens, and device, connection, and diagnostic information needed for messaging. Message identifiers and related delivery or opening status may also be processed when notifications arrive or are opened.

We do not associate push tokens with your local player nicknames or scores, or provide those tokens to Meta for advertising attribution.

Notification alerts require your permission. You can allow or disable them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Rollweave. Disabling alerts does not affect native gameplay. The system notification setting does not necessarily stop Firebase initialization, registration token maintenance, or all technical communications.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. File Import, Sharing, and Support Email

When you import a file, the App uses the system file picker to access the file you select.

When you share a game, the App provides the relevant game name and rules to the sharing destination you choose through the system share sheet. Personal information that you enter in a game name or rule may be included in the shared file. Recipients and sharing services handle that content under their own practices.

If you contact us by email, we process your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide to respond and offer support. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive information unrelated to your request.

6. Information Recipients and International Processing

Information may be processed by the following recipients to provide the features described above:

  • The privacy policy address service and policy hosting provider;
  • Apple and Google/Firebase for push notifications;
  • Meta for advertising measurement enabled with your permission; and
  • Sharing services you select and email providers used for support correspondence.

These services may process information outside your country or region. Where required, we will apply appropriate safeguards and provide additional information or obtain consent for international processing.

We may also disclose information as necessary to meet applicable legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, or protect users and service security.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local game data is generally retained until you delete the relevant content or delete the App. Deleting a game within the App may not remove earlier scores, snapshots, or exported files.

Use iOS "Delete App" to remove the App and its local data. "Offload App" may preserve data. Device backups, exported files, and data stored in an external browser must be managed separately.

We retain information under our control only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, address security issues, or meet applicable obligations. Third-party services apply their own retention and deletion practices as described in their policies.

We determine retention periods for records under our control based on the purpose of collection, whether a support request or security investigation remains open, and applicable legal requirements. When records are no longer needed for those purposes, we delete or anonymize them. Records needed for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation may be retained for the duration of that need.

Website hosting providers, Apple, Google/Firebase, and Meta may retain records under their own applicable practices and obligations. We do not promise a uniform deletion schedule across these independent services. You may contact us for help identifying the relevant recipient and submitting a deletion request.

Deleting the App or withdrawing permission does not automatically delete information already processed by third parties. You may contact us with a data request. We will handle information within our responsibility and, where applicable, assist with requests to relevant services.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information; restrict or object to processing; and withdraw consent.

You can manage tracking and notification permissions through system settings, manage local data through app and system controls, or submit a privacy request using the contact address below. We may request reasonable information necessary to verify and fulfill your request while protecting data security.

9. Children's Privacy and Information Security

The App does not require players to provide their real name or age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us through the App, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We use security measures appropriate to the information and processing risks. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Please protect access to your device and take care when sharing information.

10. Policy Updates and Contact

We will update this policy and the "Last Updated" date when our features or information practices change. Where additional notice or consent is required, we will provide it in accordance with applicable requirements.
